abstract: We introduce the Mondrian kernel, a fast random feature approximation to
  the Laplace kernel. It is suitable for both batch and online learning, and admits
  a fast kernel-width-selection procedure as the random features can be re-used efficiently
  for all kernel widths. The features are constructed by sampling trees via a Mondrian
  process [Roy and Teh, 2009], and we highlight the connection to Mondrian forests
  [Lakshminarayanan et al., 2014], where trees are also sampled via a Mondrian process,
  but fit independently. This link provides a new insight into the relationship between
  kernel methods and random forests.
