abstract: An iterative procedure introduced in MacKay’s evidence framework is often
  used for estimating the hyper-parameter in empirical Bayes. Despite its effectiveness,
  the procedure has stayed primarily as a heuristic to date. This paper formally investigates
  the mathematical nature of this procedure and justifies it as a well-principled
  algorithm framework. This framework, which we call the MacKay algorithm, is shown
  to be closely related to the EM algorithm under certain Gaussian assumption.
