abstract: We discuss a variant of Thompson sampling for nonparametric reinforcement
  learning in a countable classes of general stochastic environments. These environments
  can be non-Markov, non-ergodic, and partially observable. We show that Thompson
  sampling learns the environment classin the sense that (1) asymptotically its value
  converges to the optimal value in mean and (2) given a recoverability assumption
  regret is sublinear.
