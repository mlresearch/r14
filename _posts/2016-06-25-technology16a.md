abstract: ?We consider the sensitivity of causal identification to small perturbations
  in the input. A long line of work culminating in papers by Shpitser and Pearl and
  Huang and Valtorta led to a complete procedure for the causal identification problem.
  In our main result in this paper, we show that the identification function computed
  by these procedures is in some cases extremely unstable numerically. Specifically,
  the "condition number" of causal identification can be of the order of ?(exp(n^{0.49}))
  on an identifiable semi-Markovian model with n visible nodes. That is, in order
  to give an output accurate to d bits, the empirical probabilities of the observable
  events need to be obtained to accuracy d+?(n^{0.49}) bits.
