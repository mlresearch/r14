abstract: We consider binary pairwise graphical models and provide an exact characterization
  (necessary and sufficient conditions observing signs of potentials) of tightness
  for the LP relaxation on the triplet-consistent polytope of the MAP inference problem,
  by forbidding an odd-K5 (complete graph on 5 variables with all edges repulsive)
  as a signed minor in the signed suspension graph. This captures signs of both singleton
  and edge potentials in a compact and efficiently testable condition, and improves
  significantly on earlier results. We provide other results on tightness of LP relaxations
  by forbidding minors, draw connections and suggest paths for future research.
