abstract: 'We apply a wild bootstrap method to the Lancaster three-variable interaction
  measure in order to detect factorisation of the joint distribution on three variables
  forming a stationary random process, for which the existing permutation bootstrap
  method fails. As in the i.i.d. case, the Lancaster test is found to outperform existing
  tests in cases for which two independent variables individually have a weak influence
  on a third, but that when considered jointly the influence is strong. The main contributions
  of this paper are twofold: first, we prove that the Lancaster statistic satisfies
  the conditions required to estimate the quantiles of the null distribution using
  the wild bootstrap; second, the manner in which this is proved is novel, simpler
  than existing methods, and can further be applied to other statistics.'
